Research

How can intelligent systems learn from imperfect information?

My research investigates how intelligent systems learn, represent, integrate and reason from heterogeneous, incomplete and uncertain information. I work across the full arc from theoretical foundations to applied systems — with computer vision as a primary domain of application.

∿

Representation & Uncertainty

Probabilistic models for learning rich, structured representations from imperfect, multi-modal data.

⇌

Transfer & Generalization

Zero-shot and few-shot learning — enabling systems to generalize beyond the boundaries of training data.

⊕

Information Fusion

Integrating heterogeneous modalities and sources for robust reasoning under partial observability.
